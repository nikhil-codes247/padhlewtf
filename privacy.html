PADHLE.WTF Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

PADHLE.WTF ("we", "us", "our") operates the website and application at https://padhle.wtf (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, display name, and avatar.
  • Study Data: Chapters, topics, planner tasks, study sessions, revisions, notes, doubts, flashcard progress, and practice question attempts that you create or interact with.
  • Preferences: Theme (light/dark), reminder settings, daily study targets, and calendar preferences.
  • Payment Information: We do not collect or store credit card numbers. Payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information may be logged for security and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your study workspace and sync your data across devices.
  • To personalize your dashboard, planner, and revision recommendations.
  • To send account-related communications (password reset links, payment receipts).
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • To improve the app based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Row Level Security

All study data is tied to your account. Row Level Security ensures that one student can never read or write another student's data. Your sessions, notes, planner tasks, and revisions are accessible only to you when signed in.

5. Payments

Full access is a one-time purchase of ₹199 processed by Razorpay. PADHLE.WTF does not store your card number or payment credentials. Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant and handles payment processing on their secure servers. After a successful payment, Razorpay notifies our server, which activates full access on your account.

6. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account exists.
  • If you delete your account, your personal data and study records are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Backup exports you create remain in your possession until you delete them.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: View and export your account data from the Account page.
  • Correct: Update your username, display name, and avatar at any time from Settings.
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data permanently from Settings.
  • Object: Object to processing of your data for purposes other than providing the service.

8. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use the site. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Children

PADHLE.WTF is intended for students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify you through the app.
