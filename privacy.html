PADHLE.WTF Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Last updated: 26 November 2026

PADHLE.WTF is a study workspace for CBSE Class 10 students. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to https://padhle.wtf and the PADHLE.WTF web app (the “Service”).

1. Who we are

PADHLE.WTF (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Service. For any privacy question, data request, or complaint, contact support@padhle.wtf. You can also reach the founder directly at founder@padhle.wtf.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email/password accounts: email address, username, display name, avatar, and a securely hashed password handled by our authentication provider. We never see or store your plain-text password.
  • Google Sign-In: your name, email address, Google profile picture URL, and a Google account identifier. See section 3.

Study data you create

  • Subjects, chapters and topics you select or tick off; planner tasks; timed study sessions; revision passes; notes and doubts; flashcard progress and practice-question attempts.
  • Preferences such as theme, reminder settings, daily study target, and calendar choices.

Payment information

  • Full access is a one-time ₹199 purchase. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive a payment identifier, order identifier, amount, status, and the email/contact details needed to match the payment to your account.
  • We do not receive or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or bank credentials.

Technical and security data

  • IP address, browser type, device and operating-system information, request timestamps, and error logs, generated by our hosting and database providers for security, abuse prevention, and reliability.
  • We do not use this data for advertising, behavioural profiling, or sale.

3. Google Sign-In and Google user data

You can create an account or sign in with Google. When you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares basic profile information with us: your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a Google account identifier. We use this information only to:

  • create and authenticate your PADHLE.WTF account;
  • display your name and avatar inside the app and on your own progress/share cards; and
  • keep your study data linked to the correct account across devices.

We do not request or access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, location, or any other Google service. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, sale, or transfer to third parties, and we do not allow humans to read it except where necessary for security, support at your request, or legal compliance.

PADHLE.WTF’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke PADHLE.WTF’s access at any time from your Google Account’s security settings. Deleting your PADHLE.WTF account deletes the Google profile data we stored. See sections 6 and 7.

4. How we use your information

  • Provide the Service: render your syllabus, planner, timer, revision ladder, notes and analytics, and sync them across devices.
  • Personalise: choose the subjects shown on your dashboard and build revision/planner suggestions from your own activity.
  • Payments and entitlements: verify a Razorpay payment server-side and unlock full access on the correct account until 10 March 2027.
  • Communicate: send account emails such as email confirmation, password reset, payment/receipt information, and responses to support requests.
  • Secure and improve: detect fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, debug errors, and understand aggregate usage so we can improve the product.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your study data, notes or doubts for advertising. We do not train advertising profiles on Google user data.

5. Legal bases and consent

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (providing the study workspace and the paid entitlement); our legitimate interests in keeping the Service secure and reliable; your consent (for optional cookies and for Google Sign-In when you choose it); and legal obligations (for example, tax and payment records). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your theme and preferences, remember whether you have answered the cookie notice, and store the app’s local cache so it opens quickly. Essential storage is required for the Service to work. Optional storage is used only after you choose “Accept all” in the cookie notice; choosing “Essential only” keeps optional storage off. You can clear site data at any time from your browser settings.

We do not run advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics that profile you across other websites.

7. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — authentication, database hosting and row-level security for account and study data.
  • Cloudflare Pages — static site hosting and serverless functions that verify payments.
  • Razorpay — payment processing for the one-time ₹199 purchase.
  • Google — only if you choose Google Sign-In, for authentication and basic profile data.
  • Zoho Mail — email mailbox for support and transactional email.

These providers process data on our instructions and under their own security and data-protection terms. We do not share your study content with them beyond what is needed to run the Service. We may disclose data if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of students, or in connection with a reorganisation of the Service (subject to this policy).

8. Security and isolation

Account and study records live in Supabase with Row Level Security enabled, so one signed-in student cannot read or write another student’s rows. Payment signatures are verified server-side using a secret that never reaches the browser. Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Account and study data are kept while your account exists.
  • Deleting your account from the Account page permanently deletes your authentication user, profile, subject selection, and study records from our live systems, subject to short-lived backups and records we must keep by law (for example, payment/tax records).
  • “Clear study data” in Settings deletes planner tasks, sessions, revisions, notes and completion ticks while keeping your profile and subject selection.
  • Payment records may be retained for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Backup files you export remain on your own device under your control.

10. Your rights

  • Access and portability: view your data in the app and export your study data from the Account page.
  • Correction: update your username, display name and avatar from Settings or Account.
  • Deletion: delete your account and associated data from the Account page. You can also email support@padhle.wtf.
  • Objection and restriction: object to processing that is not necessary to provide the Service.
  • Complaint: contact us first at support@padhle.wtf; you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We respond to verified requests within a reasonable period and may need to confirm your identity before acting.

11. Children and students

PADHLE.WTF is built for CBSE Class 10 students, who are often minors. We collect only the data needed to run the study workspace. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact support@padhle.wtf and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than India. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers (such as standard contractual clauses). By using the Service you understand that your data may be processed outside your state or country.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, announced in the app. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
